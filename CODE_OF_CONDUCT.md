# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Atlas community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness.
- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing professionalism in discussions and reviews.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling or disruptive behavior.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, discussions, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all official Atlas spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories
- Discussions
- Issues
- Pull Requests
- Community communication channels
- Official events

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the Atlas maintainers.

Reports will be handled confidentially whenever possible.

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community maintainers may take actions including:

1. Correction
2. Warning
3. Temporary restriction
4. Permanent removal from the community

Actions should be proportionate to the severity and frequency of violations.
