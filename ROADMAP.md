# Atlas Roadmap

> **A long-term roadmap for building the world's most trusted knowledge infrastructure.**

---

# Purpose

This roadmap defines the strategic evolution of the Atlas ecosystem.

Rather than focusing only on software releases, it outlines the long-term progression of Atlas as a standards-driven platform for trustworthy knowledge infrastructure.

---

# Guiding Principles

Every milestone in this roadmap shall support the Atlas Mission, Vision, Manifesto, Philosophy, and Principles.

Short-term implementation shall never compromise long-term sustainability.

# Phase 1 — Foundation

Objective

Establish the constitutional and architectural foundation of Atlas.

Repositories

- Atlas-Core
- Atlas-Foundation
- Atlas-Standards
- Atlas-Templates

Success Criteria

- Constitutional documents completed.
- Architecture frozen.
- Repository model established.
- Governance model approved.

# Long-Term Success Indicators

Atlas aims to achieve:

- Wide adoption of Atlas standards.
- Multiple independent implementations.
- Sustainable contributor community.
- Transparent governance.
- Long-term interoperability.
- Global scientific collaboration.

---

# Release Strategy

Atlas repositories release independently.

Standards evolve independently from implementations.

Compatibility is maintained through:

- Compatibility Matrix
- Conformance Test Suites
- Semantic Versioning

---

# Roadmap Governance

The roadmap is reviewed quarterly.

Major strategic changes require:

- RFC
- ADR
- Community Review
- Approval

---

# Vision 2036

By 2036 Atlas seeks to become the foundational infrastructure upon which trustworthy knowledge systems are built.

Success will be measured not by the number of repositories, but by the quality, interoperability, sustainability, and real-world impact of the ecosystem.

---

# Closing Statement

The Atlas roadmap represents a long-term commitment rather than a fixed schedule.

It provides strategic direction while allowing implementations to evolve responsibly.
