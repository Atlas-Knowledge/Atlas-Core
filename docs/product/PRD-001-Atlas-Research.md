# PRD-001 — Atlas Research

Version: 0.1

Status: Draft

---

# Product Name

Atlas Research

---

# Tagline

From Papers to Discoveries.

---

# Mission

Enable researchers to transform scientific literature into structured knowledge and discover hidden relationships, unanswered questions, and promising research directions.

---

# Problem

Researchers spend enormous time reading papers, organizing notes, and connecting ideas manually.

Important relationships are often missed.

Knowledge remains fragmented across thousands of documents.

---

# Target Users

Primary

- Researchers
- PhD Students
- Professors
- Scientists

Secondary

- Engineers
- Physicians
- Analysts

---

# MVP Goal

A researcher uploads research papers.

Atlas automatically produces:

- Knowledge Graph
- Entities
- Relationships
- Evidence
- Research Summary

---

# Inputs

- PDF
- Markdown
- Plain Text
- URLs

---

# Outputs

- Extracted Entities
- Research Topics
- Citations
- Relationships
- Evidence Map
- Open Questions

---

# Future Outputs

- Contradictions
- Hypotheses
- Discovery Suggestions
- Research Roadmap
- Confidence Scores

---

# Success Metrics

A researcher can analyze an entire collection of papers in minutes instead of days.

Atlas reveals information that would be difficult to discover manually.

---

# Non Goals

Atlas is not:

- A chatbot
- A document storage system
- A search engine

Atlas is a Discovery System.

---
